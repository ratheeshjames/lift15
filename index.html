First time lifting?

You don't need to know the exercises yet. Lift 15 now includes a built-in exercise guide and a weekly preview so you can learn at your own pace.

Before your first workout
  • Start with a light, comfortable dumbbell.
  • Open How to do it whenever a movement is unfamiliar.
  • Prioritize smooth, controlled reps over heavy weight.
  • Stop if you feel sharp or unusual pain.
